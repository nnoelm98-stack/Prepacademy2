Complex Numbers
Master operations with complex numbers — addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division — and the cyclic powers of i that the SAT uses in every complex number problem.

What is i and how do powers cycle?
The imaginary unit i is defined as √(−1), so i² = −1. Powers of i cycle with period 4 — this is the most important pattern to memorize.

Operations with complex numbers
A complex number has the form a + bi where a is the real part and b is the imaginary part.
Adding & Subtracting
Combine real parts and imaginary parts separately.
(3+2i)+(5−4i) = 8−2i
(7+i)−(3−6i) = 4+7i
Multiplying — use FOIL then replace i²=−1
Distribute (FOIL), then substitute i² = −1 to simplify.
(2+3i)(1−4i)
= 2−8i+3i−12i²
= 2−5i−12(−1)
= 14−5i
Complex conjugate
The conjugate of a+bi is a−bi. Their product is always real: (a+bi)(a−bi) = a²+b².
Conjugate of 3+4i is 3−4i
(3+4i)(3−4i) = 9+16 = 25
Dividing — multiply by conjugate
Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator to eliminate i from the denominator.
(1+2i)/(3+i)
= (1+2i)(3−i)/[(3+i)(3−i)]
= (3−i+6i−2i²)/(9+1)
= (5+5i)/10 = ½+½i

SAT strategy tips
Cycle trick: divide exponent by 4, use remainder
For any iⁿ, divide n by 4 and use the remainder: 0→1, 1→i, 2→−1, 3→−i. This handles any power instantly. i¹⁰⁰ → 100÷4=25 r0 → i¹⁰⁰ = 1.
Multiply by conjugate to divide
To simplify a fraction with i in the denominator, always multiply top and bottom by the conjugate. This clears the imaginary part from the denominator and leaves a real number there.
i² = −1, always
Every time i² appears (from FOIL or anywhere else), replace it with −1 immediately. This is the single most critical step in complex number arithmetic — missing it is the most common error.
Real and imaginary parts stay separate
When adding complex numbers, keep real and imaginary terms separate. (3+2i) + (5+4i) = 8+6i — never combine a real term with an imaginary term into a single number.

Problem

Expand: (a+bi)² = a² + 2abi + b²i² = (a²−b²) + 2abi. Match real parts: a²−b²=21. Match imaginary parts: 2ab=20 → ab=10. Solve the system.
