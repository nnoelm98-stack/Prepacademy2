Circles
Master the standard equation of a circle, arc length, sector area, inscribed angles, and the key circle theorems the SAT tests — all in the coordinate plane.
13 min read3 practice questions+20 pts on completion
Circle equations and properties
Standard equation of a circle
(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²
Center = (h, k) · Radius = r. Watch signs: (x − 3)² has center x = 3, not −3.
Arc length & sector area
Arc = (θ/360) · 2πr
Sector = (θ/360) · πr²
θ = central angle in degrees. Both are fractions of the full circle.
Inscribed angle theorem
Inscribed angle = ½ · central angle
An inscribed angle is half the central angle that subtends the same arc.
Tangent line to circle
Tangent ⊥ radius at point of tangency
A tangent line is perpendicular to the radius drawn to the point of contact. Use Pythagorean theorem for tangent length problems.
Completing the square
x² + y² + Dx + Ey + F = 0
Group x and y terms, complete the square on each group to find center and radius.
Semicircle inscribed angle
Angle in semicircle = 90°
Any angle inscribed in a semicircle (with the diameter as its side) is always a right angle.
Worked example — completing the square
Find the center and radius of: x² + y² − 6x + 4y − 3 = 0

Group: (x² − 6x) + (y² + 4y) = 3
Complete: (x² − 6x + 9) + (y² + 4y + 4) = 3 + 9 + 4
(x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 16
Center: (3, −2) · Radius: 4
SAT strategy tips
Center sign trap — always check
(x − 3)² + (y + 5)² = 49 has center (3, −5) — NOT (−3, 5). The signs in the equation are opposite to the center coordinates. Rewrite (y + 5)² as (y − (−5))² to see it clearly.
Completing the square for general form
When the circle equation is expanded (x² + y² + Dx + Ey = F), complete the square. Add (D/2)² and (E/2)² to both sides. The right side becomes the radius squared.
Inscribed angle = half the arc it intercepts
An inscribed angle equals half the central angle subtending the same arc. If the arc is 80°, the inscribed angle is 40°. A diameter subtends a 180° arc — so any inscribed angle on a diameter = 90°.
Tangent + radius = right angle
When a tangent meets a radius, they form a 90° angle. Draw this right angle and use the Pythagorean theorem to find tangent length, if given the radius and distance from center to external point.

Problem
A circle has center O and radius 9. Points A and B are on the circle. The central angle AOB measures 140°.
Arc length = (central angle/360) × 2πr = (140/360) × 2π(9). The inscribed angle that subtends the same arc = half the central angle = 70°.
